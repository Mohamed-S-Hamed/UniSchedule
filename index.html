How it works

Four quick steps from selection to a shareable image.

01

Choose

Pick your academic year, then your group. Add a practical section if you attend one.

02

Generate

UniSchedule filters the verified timetable data locally and builds your week.

03

Review

Switch between weekly grid and daily agenda, filter by FTF or VCR, and check conflicts.

04

Export

Download a high-resolution PNG in a light or dark theme, laid out like a printed academic planner.

  • FTF means on campus. VCR means online. Sessions whose mode is not printed in the source show “Mode not specified”.
  • Lectures belong to a group (A or B). Practical sessions belong to a section (S01–S07) and are listed per year; the source does not link sections to groups.
  • Times use the 24-hour clock. Session 1 starts at 08:00 and each session lasts one hour.
  • Overlapping sessions are never hidden. They are shown together and flagged.

UniSchedule is an independent student tool. It is not endorsed by, or affiliated with, any university.

Timetable data source: “IT Schedule Fall Semester 2026–2027”, Faculty of Computers and Information Technology, Egyptian E-Learning University. Always confirm changes with the official timetable.
